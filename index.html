We all want to be decent people. We love our neighbourhoods. We value our friends. We care for our environment. We serve our country.

But being loving people means we also have to be thinking people.

We need to build our neighbourhoods on true dialogue. We value friends who are truthful with us. We care for our environment using accurate measurements. We serve our country by living truthfully.

Truth means we live in reality, and especially at a university, we need to learn about the reality we live in.
