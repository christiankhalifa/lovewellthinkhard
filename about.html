About us

We’re a group of local Christians who love our community and love dialogue.

We think good ideas are worth talking about and good questions are worth asking, because God gave us minds that can appreciate both.

We’re on campus because we want to talk to truth-seekers like us who want to love our communities well by thinking hard. We want to ask the tough questions and hear your answers.

Questions we’d love to talk about

  • Science
  • Sin
  • Faith
  • Atheism
  • Religion
  • Gender
  • Politics
  • Sexuality
  • Anything else you want to talk about

How we talk with people

  1. Ask the tough questions

    Science, faith, gender, politics, and anything else on your mind.

  2. Hear your answers

    We want to know what you think, and why.

  3. Think it through

    Good ideas are worth talking about. Good questions are worth asking.

  4. Keep talking

    On campus, on Discord, or by email.
